# Scene-to-audio examples

Use these as adaptable starting points. They are not verified generated outputs. English descriptions below are a practical choice, not a claim that Suno requires English.

## Underwater exploration music

For a calm diving scene with background music:

> Instrumental ambient game soundtrack for slow underwater exploration by a lone diver. Quiet, spacious, mysterious but reassuring. Warm sustained synth pads, soft rounded bass, sparse glass-like notes, gentle low-frequency pulses, very restrained percussion. Low intensity, stable texture, leave space for gameplay sounds. No vocals, lyrics, spoken words, or choir. No sudden rises, dramatic drops, or sharp ending. Loop-friendly atmosphere. Target approximately 90 seconds.

The 90 seconds is illustrative. Use the task's target; measure the result. “Loop-friendly” is an arrangement request, not verified seamless playback.

## Aquarium / gentle water ambience

When the user wants environmental sound rather than music, use Sounds by default:

> Quiet aquarium ambience heard close to the water: gentle continuous water movement, soft irregular bubbles, subtle filtration hum, calm enclosed atmosphere. Natural environmental texture only. No melody, instruments, rhythm, voices, dramatic splashes, or sharp transients. Consistent background level, suitable for repeated ambient playback. Target approximately 30 seconds.

An underwater listener, an aquarium visitor, and someone standing on a beach hear different things. Use the scene's listener perspective. Do not add surface waves by default to a deep underwater scene. If the current product cannot produce a usable nonmusical result, report that rather than relabeling music as ambience.

## Discrete game effect

> One short muted underwater pressure pulse with a soft bubble release, close perspective, clear onset and short natural decay. Isolated sound effect, no music, no voices, no repeated pulses, no background bed. Target approximately one second.

Exact duration, onset alignment, isolation, and clean silence must be checked on the actual file. Do not promise a production-ready one-shot from a text request alone. If an edit is necessary, describe it before treating a changed asset as satisfying the original request.

## Other projects

Replace setting, emotional tone, timbre, energy and gameplay role from the current project. For a puzzle game, prioritize unobtrusive concentration; for a chase, the task may call for urgency and rhythmic drive. The diving examples must not become a global style constraint.
